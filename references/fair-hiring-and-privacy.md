# Fair Hiring and Privacy Notes

This skill is designed to support structured recruiting decisions, not to replace accountable human judgment.

## Use Job-Relevant Information Only
Assess qualifications, experience, scope, outcomes, skills, and other evidence relevant to the role. Avoid using protected or sensitive personal attributes as hiring signals.

## Minimize Personal Data
CVs often contain more personal data than is necessary for assessment. Do not reproduce or highlight personal contact details, home addresses, identity numbers, tax information, or other unrelated personal data.

## Avoid Proxy Inference
Do not infer protected or sensitive attributes from names, photographs, schools, locations, languages, memberships, or hobbies.

## Distinguish Missing Evidence from Missing Capability
A CV is an incomplete document. Use `Not Evidenced` when the resume does not establish a requirement, and convert that gap into a targeted interview question when appropriate.

## Human Review
Treat the recommendation as decision support. Material hiring decisions should remain subject to appropriate human review and applicable employment, anti-discrimination, privacy, and AI-governance requirements in the relevant jurisdiction.
