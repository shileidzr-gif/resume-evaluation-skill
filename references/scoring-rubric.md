# Scoring Rubric

## Dimension Rating
Score each weighted dimension from 0 to 5:

- **5 — Exceptional:** strong, specific evidence clearly exceeds the role requirement.
- **4 — Strong:** clear evidence fully meets the role requirement.
- **3 — Adequate:** generally meets the role requirement with acceptable evidence.
- **2 — Partial:** some relevant evidence, but a meaningful gap remains.
- **1 — Weak:** minimal evidence or a major gap.
- **0 — None / mismatch:** no relevant evidence or clear contradiction.

Weighted score = sum of `(rating / 5) × dimension weight`.

## Default Score Bands
- **85–100:** Strong Advance
- **70–84:** Advance
- **55–69:** Hold
- **Below 55:** Reject

## Hard-Gate Override
A `Not Met` result on a genuinely critical Hard Gate may cap the recommendation at **Hold** or **Reject**, even when the weighted score is otherwise high.

A `Not Evidenced` result should normally become an interview verification point rather than an automatic rejection unless documentary proof is inherently required.

## Weight Adjustment
The default weights are a starting point, not a fixed formula. Adjust them when the role clearly places unusual emphasis on areas such as:
- regulated certification,
- local employment-law expertise,
- language fluency,
- people leadership,
- P&L ownership,
- technical depth,
- sales quota ownership,
- country-specific operating experience.

State material weight changes when they affect the decision.
