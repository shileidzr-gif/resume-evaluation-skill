# Use Case — Operations Manager

> Fictional example for demonstration only.

## Target Role
Operations Manager for a regional last-mile logistics network.

### Key Requirements
- 5+ years of operations-management experience
- Experience managing frontline teams and supervisors
- Ownership of service KPIs, productivity, cost, and safety
- Ability to improve processes and stabilize underperforming sites
- Strong cross-functional communication

## Candidate Summary
- 8 years in logistics operations
- Currently manages a site handling 45,000 parcels/day
- Leads 6 supervisors and approximately 140 frontline employees
- Owns SLA, productivity, labor planning, quality, and safety metrics
- Reduced missort rate by 22% and overtime hours by 15% over 12 months
- Previously supported two new-site launches

## Example Evaluation

### Overall Recommendation
- **Fit Score:** 91/100
- **Decision:** Strong Advance
- **Rationale:** Strong scale, quantified operational outcomes, and direct ownership of the core metrics required by the role.

### Hard Gate Check
| Requirement | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 5+ years operations experience | Met | 8 years in logistics operations |
| Frontline team leadership | Met | 6 supervisors / ~140 frontline employees |
| KPI ownership | Met | Owns SLA, productivity, labor, quality, and safety |
| Process improvement | Met | Missort -22%; overtime -15% |
| Multi-site / launch exposure | Partial | Supported two launches but current direct ownership is single-site |

### Evidence Highlights
- **E3:** Site scale of 45,000 parcels/day and 140 frontline employees
- **E3:** Quantified quality and labor improvements
- **E3:** Direct ownership of core operating KPIs
- **E2:** New-site launch experience is relevant but ownership level needs verification

### Strengths
1. Strong evidence of operating at meaningful scale
2. Quantified productivity and quality improvements
3. Clear people-management scope
4. Relevant launch and stabilization experience

### Risks / Verification Points
- Verify direct P&L or budget ownership
- Clarify whether improvement results were personally led or delivered by a broader project team
- Test ability to manage multiple sites if regional scope is required

### Interview Focus
1. Root-cause analysis under service pressure
2. Labor planning and overtime control
3. Supervisor performance management
4. Safety incidents and corrective actions
5. Budget and cost ownership

### Targeted Interview Questions
1. Walk me through the missort reduction from baseline to result. What did you personally change?
2. How do you build a weekly labor plan when volume is volatile?
3. Tell me about a supervisor who was underperforming. What actions did you take?
4. Describe the most serious service failure you managed. How did you recover the operation?
5. What cost lines did you directly own, and how did you track them?
6. In a new-site launch, which decisions were yours versus the project team's?

### Final Hiring View
Proceed quickly. The candidate has unusually strong evidence of scale and measurable operational impact. The main remaining questions are budget ownership and readiness for multi-site scope.
